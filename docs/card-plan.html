Merged into main · 30 Oct 2026

Card Plan

Two batches: 22 new spells and 10 new items, all real Deadlock items with their real art. Also fixes for cards that broke the design rules, balance changes, and 5 rebuilt starter decks. The game now has 36 spells and 53 items. Every number here comes from 400 games where the AI played itself.

Merged

Core rules now in docs/card-design.md

1

An active item in Deadlock is always a Spell. A passive item is always Equipment. No exceptions.

2

Keep the main game's flavor. Every card uses its item's current name, does what the item does in Deadlock, and keeps the item's shop slot (Weapon, Spirit or Vitality) and tier.

Tiers map from shop price: 800 / 1,600 / 3,200 / 6,400 souls → T1 / T2 / T3 / T4. Spells top out at 5–6 souls, so a T4 active item becomes a 5–6 soul spell.

What's wrong now

  • 3 cards are the wrong type. Healbane and Active Reload are passive in Deadlock but are spells here. Colossus is active in Deadlock but is equipment here.
  • 7 cards use old or removed item names: Debuff Remover, Silence Glyph, Curse, Improved Cooldown, Improved Burst, Improved Bullet Armor and Improved Spirit Armor.
  • The 5 starter decks use only 23 of 60 spells and items.
  • No spell draws cards, hits every enemy, or moves heroes around. The game can already pull, swap, apply Dome and apply Rooted, but no card does.

Echo Shard · Mystic Burst · Crippling Headshot

Moved to their Deadlock tiers. Echo Shard costs 4 → 5. Mystic Burst costs 3 → 2. Crippling Headshot costs 5 → 7, applies −2 resists instead of −1, and gains +1 Bullet Power

Burst Fire also tests weak (30%), but its effect is strong on paper. The AI may be equipping it on bench heroes that never attack. I'll check that before changing the card.

Where it stands

Everything on this page is built, tested (1,023 tests), visible in the game, and merged into main as commit 2e01a08. It has not been pushed.

Canon item types and tiers were checked against the Deadlock wiki on 30 Oct 2026; the item API was unreachable. Win rates come from 400 games of the game's AI against itself, with random hero drafts. A card is counted once per game, for the side that played it.
