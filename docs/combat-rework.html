Lane Combos and Ricochet

Your three ideas from this morning, written up as rules so you can check them before I build: attacks per hero with retreat as the combo engine, Ricochet that spreads your on-hit debuffs to the whole enemy board, and a proper pictogram for every status.

Awaiting your OK

1 · Attacks are per hero

Today: one attack a turn, whoever is in front. Proposed: every hero may attack once a turn while it stands in the Active slot. Retreat (2 souls) swaps who stands there, so a turn can chain attackers.

Haze attacks3 bullet→ Retreat −2→ Shiv attacks2 bullet + Bleed→ Retreat −2→ Abrams steps inthe tank takes the rival's turn

Turn 5, 5 souls: two attacks and a wall in front, 1 soul left for a card. Later, with retreats made cheaper, all four heroes swing and the tank still ends up in front.

The rules

  • A hero attacks at most once a turn. One that attacked, retreated and came back cannot swing again.
  • A hero still does its skill or its attack, never both. Bench heroes still only cast.
  • No attack on Turn 1, as now. A hero that steps up because the Active fell may attack, as now.
  • Retreat stays at 2 souls by default. The whole combo is paid for in souls, so the soul curve (1 → 10) is what limits it early.

How retreats get down to zero

Real Deadlock mobility items, each a passive or active as the rules require. The retreating hero's own gear sets its price.

Already in the game and now combo pieces: Warp Stone and Rescue Beam (free swaps), Mirage's Traveler (a 2-soul free retreat), Phantom Strike (drag their caster forward, then chain into it).

What it costs to build, and the risk

  • Engine: the attack gate moves from "one this turn" to "this hero this turn"; a Retreat-cost rule that reads the Active's gear; a Swift status for the free-retreat cards.
  • The AI only looks one move ahead, so it won't see attack → retreat → attack on its own. It needs to value a fresh attacker in front, or it will fall behind a human who does.
  • Damage per turn goes up, so games get shorter. Plan: build it, run the 400-game sim, then tune Retreat cost or hero HP, not the combo itself.
  • Rule text in the README, the engine doc, the tutorial's first lesson and the fuzz oracle all say "one attack a turn" and get rewritten.

2 · Ricochet spreads the hit

Ricochet N: after the bearer's attack lands, every other enemy hero takes N bullet damage, and the attack's on-hit debuffs land on them too. It fires on every swing, so Extra Attacks ricochet again.

What spreads, what doesn't

Spreads to the whole boardStays on the main target
Bleed (Toxic Bullets, Shiv's knives), Bullet and Spirit Resist shreds, Root (Slowing Bullets), Bullet / Spirit Power down (Inhibitor), Mirage's Djinn's Mark, the stolen HP of Siphon BulletsThe bearer's own heals (lifesteal, Restorative Shot, Frenzy), Fixation's extra swing, Tesla's chain, the kill bounty and exp

Where it comes from

  • Ricochet (equipment, Weapon T4, 7): Ricochet 2 while worn. Today it deals 2 to the bench with no debuffs; it becomes the keyword.
  • Split Shot (spell, Weapon T2, 3): reworked to "Your Active gains Ricochet 2 this turn." The one-shot volley it does today becomes a swing that carries your gear.
  • Later, if you want it: a hero whose gun bounces by nature. None of the 19 has that in Deadlock, so I'd leave heroes alone for now.

With per-hero attacks this is the payoff of a gun combo: Shiv with Ricochet bleeds everyone, then Haze swings twice into a shredded board.

3 · A pictogram for every status

Today 5 statuses have a drawn mark (Fixation, Dome, Rooted, Rabbit, Last Stand); the other 24 are text chips. Every status gets its own SVG mark in the same 16-unit line-art style, on its class colour: green buff, red debuff, ink for the rest. The same drawing lands as the sticker when the status is applied.

Hover already works: pointing at a status chip (or tapping it on a phone) shows what it does and "N turns left". I'll make sure every new mark keeps that, and add the turns to the sticker's flash too.
